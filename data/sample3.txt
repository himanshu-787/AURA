Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball team sport played between two teams of eleven players on a large field featuring a 22-yard central pitch. The game revolves around alternating turns of batting and bowling, where the batting side tries to score as many runs as possible by hitting the ball and running between the wickets or crossing the boundary, while the fielding and bowling side tries to dismiss the batters. Overseen by the International Cricket Council, cricket is played in multiple formats—ranging from fast-paced T20s and One Day Internationals (ODIs) to traditional five-day Test matches. Beyond mere physical activity, it fosters discipline, teamwork, and strategy, holding a massive, passionate following across the Commonwealth and particularly in nations like India
