Space is the vast, nearly empty expanse that begins roughly 62 miles (100 kilometers) above Earth's atmosphere at the Kármán line. Far from being completely empty, this cold, dark vacuum is filled with thinly spread gas, dust, cosmic radiation, dark matter, and billions of galaxies containing stars, planets, and black holes. Governed by the expansion of the universe since the Big Bang, outer space remains a frontier of constant scientific discovery, explored through powerful telescopes and spacecraft managed by agencies like NASA Science. You can read more about the physics and dimensions of the cosmos on Wikipedia's Outer Space page.
