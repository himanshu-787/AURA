Grid Architecture Details
Voltage Levels: The system is split into distinct voltage zones. The transmission level (Buses 1 to 5) typically operates at 132 kV, while the distribution/sub-transmission network (Buses 6 and 9 to 14) operates at 33 kV. 
Control Elements: The network features an overabundance of voltage control capabilities via its 5 generators and 5 transformers (three of which commonly model a single three-winding transformer arrangement).
